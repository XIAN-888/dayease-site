Start simply

Add a local task and choose a time if it needs one. Local tasks, short timers and basic widgets are free. No account is needed. Other information sources remain optional.

How do I prepare and save my place?

Open an item’s Get ready page, choose a starting list or add steps, and check what is ready. Prepare ahead sets a Ready by planning time; it does not schedule an alert. Choose an available duration under A short session. To pause, tap Save place, optionally enter a next step, then Pause timer. Reopen Dayease and choose Continue to use the remaining seconds; time away does not count. Preparation, pause and continuation for local items are free.

What happens if I disconnect a source?

Its imported items are removed, while local preparation and saved places stay in Settings → Your data → Saved preparation. You can inspect or remove them there without changing the original source. Continuing requires the linked item to be available. Only activity records expire after 90 days; preparation and saved places do not. Restoring a backup does not automatically reconnect sources.

Calendar and Reminders

Connect a source in Settings, grant the system permission and select the calendars or lists you want. Calendar events are read-only. Completing a current reminder updates its original list. Future repetitions are shown as read-only context even when an earlier occurrence is still unfinished.

Widgets and Live Activities

Add a widget through the iOS widget gallery. Start a timer to use its supported Live Activity. Widgets do not pop up automatically at a task's time. iOS controls refresh and delivery; check Focus, notification settings and Live Activity settings if information seems delayed.

Weather and travel

Choose a city for weather; location permission is optional. Journey estimates are requested from an event with a usable place and need your current location, a confirmed destination and a connection. Weather and routes depend on Apple's service availability. A saved weather location is not used as your journey origin.

Optional alarms

Task and routine alarms require iOS 26 or later and AlarmKit permission. They start off. Check their scheduling state in the app. Stopping a ringing alarm does not complete the task. Existing iPhone Clock alarms cannot be imported.

Pro and the free trial

Dayease Pro adds weather and journey estimates, selected Calendar/Reminders connections, optional alarms and the full companion/palette choices. The annual subscription and lifetime purchase unlock the same Pro features. Eligible users can confirm an annual 7-day free trial through Apple. It renews at the shown annual price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends; eligibility is determined by Apple. Lifetime has no trial. Local prices and terms are shown before confirmation. When Pro ends, saved data and existing alarm schedules remain. New or changed Pro configuration and paid source/weather refreshes pause. You can still turn off or delete an existing alarm, and undoing its deletion can restore the previous schedule.

Restore or cancel

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used to buy. Manage or cancel an annual subscription in Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Restoring access does not restore a separate Dayease backup. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Records and backups

Only activity receipts expire: up to 90 local days and the latest 500 records. Tasks and alarm plans stay. In Settings, export a backup before replacing data. Restoring replaces Dayease's local saved items and records and disconnects sources/notifications; it does not change original Calendar or Reminders data. Exported files are not automatically erased.
