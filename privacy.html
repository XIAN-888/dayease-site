Privacy policy

Updated November 15, 2026

Your data stays close

Dayease is provided by the developer named on its App Store listing. It does not require an account and has no advertising, analytics or custom cloud server. Saved tasks, notes, dates, source copies, preferences, weather cache, timer/alarm plans and activity records are stored in a local App Group shared by the app and its extensions.

Connections you choose

Calendar and Reminders access is optional and uses system permissions. Only your selected calendars and lists are read. Calendar events are not edited or deleted. Completing an editable current reminder updates its original list; read-only future repetitions cannot complete another occurrence. Disconnecting a source removes its current imported items from Dayease; past activity records follow their retention rule.

Weather, places and routes

City and place searches are sent to Apple Maps. Weather refreshes send the saved coordinates of your chosen city or location to Apple's WeatherKit service. Location permission is requested only after you choose a location action; you can search for a weather city instead. A location chosen for weather is saved for later weather refreshes. Disconnecting weather stops its use and clears cached weather, but retains the last city and coordinates in preferences. A requested journey estimate sends your current origin, confirmed destination and travel mode to Apple Maps. Route results are not saved to your calendar or the app's cache. Dayease does not continuously track your location. Apple's own privacy terms apply to its services.

What can appear on your screen

Widgets, Live Activities and alarms may show task or session titles on system surfaces, including a locked screen. Add or remove these surfaces and control previews, notifications and permissions in your device settings. Alarms are optional and are supported only on compatible systems. Dayease does not read the iPhone Clock app's alarms.

Retention and backups

Operation records are kept for up to 90 local calendar days and at most the latest 500 records. This limit is enforced when the app reads, saves or restores its shared data; it does not erase tasks, source reminders, alarm plans or preferences. Manually exported files and copies preserved after a storage failure are not automatically erased by that rule. Exported backups may contain titles, notes and other saved app data: share them only where you intend. Device backups and the services you choose for Files or sharing are controlled by your system/account settings and those providers. Removing the app removes its installed local data under iOS rules; separately saved or backed-up copies can remain.

Purchases

Apple handles payment for Dayease Pro. The app uses StoreKit purchase and entitlement information on your device to provide access and restore eligible purchases. The developer does not receive your payment-card details and does not operate a receipt server. The annual subscription may offer a 7-day free trial to eligible users after Apple purchase confirmation, followed by automatic renewal at the displayed annual price unless cancelled. A lifetime purchase is a separate one-time purchase. Check the purchase screen and Apple's confirmation for the applicable terms. When Pro ends, saved data and existing alarm schedules remain. New or changed Pro configuration and paid source/weather refreshes pause. You can still turn off or delete an existing alarm, and undoing its deletion can restore the previous schedule.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer and its email provider receive the message, your address and any attachments you choose to send. No backup, calendar or diagnostic file is uploaded automatically. Include only what is necessary and redact private information. Support messages are used to address your request; they are separate from the app's 90-day activity-record limit.

Your choices

You can edit or delete local tasks, clear activity records, disconnect sources, change appearance and turn optional permissions off. The app does not use Contacts, HealthKit, microphone or photo-library access in its current feature set. There is no account to delete. Contact support for questions about this policy.
